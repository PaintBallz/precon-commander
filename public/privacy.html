Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Who runs this site

Pre-Con Commander is a free hobby site for running a Magic: The Gathering Commander league with friends. It's run by one private individual, paintballz (that's the Discord username), not a company. It has no ads, no purchases and no paid features.

Questions or requests: message @paintballz on Discord, or use Suggest a feature at the bottom of the site once you're signed in.

What the site stores

  • Your account: username, email address, your password (stored only as a one-way scrypt hash, never in readable form), and the precon deck name you enter, if any.
  • League play: the leagues and pods you're in, wins, votes, packs you open and the cards in them, and the activity feed of those leagues. Other members of your leagues can see this; that's what the site is for.
  • Feature suggestions you send, which only the admin can read.

Your email is only used to send password-reset links when you ask for one. It isn't shown to other players, used for newsletters or marketing, or shared with anyone.

What it doesn't do

No analytics, no tracking pixels, no ad networks, no third-party scripts, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. Fonts are served from this site itself.

Cookies and storage

  • pcc_sid: one cookie that keeps you signed in. It holds a random session code (no personal details), lasts up to 30 days and is deleted when you log out. The site can't work without it, so there's no cookie banner.
  • Local storage in your browser keeps a few display preferences (sound on or off, Fun Packs view and pack count, open panels). It never leaves your device.

Short-lived technical data

To slow down password guessing and spam sign-ups, the server counts recent attempts per IP address in memory for up to an hour. These counts aren't written to disk and disappear when the server restarts.

Other services involved

  • Cloudflare carries traffic to the site (like any network provider, it sees your IP address and the pages you request).
  • Scryfall (scryfall.com): card and set images load from Scryfall's servers, so your browser contacts them directly.
  • An email provider delivers password-reset emails, if email is switched on.

How long data is kept

Account and league data stay until you delete your account (or the admin removes it). Sign-in sessions end after 30 days; password-reset links expire after 60 minutes.

Deleting your data

Go to Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your username, email, password and precon name, signs you out everywhere and deletes your feature suggestions. Leagues are shared games, so the pods you played and the packs you opened stay in their history (other players' standings depend on them), but your name there is replaced with Deleted player. If you'd rather everything you were part of were removed, ask the admin.

Children

The site isn't meant for anyone under 13, and sign-up requires confirming you're 13 or older. If a child under 13 has signed up, contact the admin and the account will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.

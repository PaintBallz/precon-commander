What this is

Pre-Con Commander is a free hobby site, run by one private individual (paintballz on Discord), for keeping score in a Magic: The Gathering Commander league among friends. By making an account you agree to these terms and to the Privacy Policy.

No purchases, no real cards

Nothing on the site costs money and nothing can be bought, sold or won. Packs opened here are simulations: no physical or digital cards are delivered, and pulls have no cash value.

How packs are simulated

Packs are generated at random from booster print-sheet data published by MTGJSON and card data from Scryfall; a few sets with no published sheets use a simpler generated layout. This data is compiled by the community and may contain mistakes, so the odds here are approximate and may not match real boosters.
